## 3. Event Objectives

## 5. Key Highlights

The following topics are explanations of knowledge related to the general event themes, rather than detailed records of individual speakers' presentations.

### 5.1. Cloud Computing

Cloud computing plays an important role in building, deploying, and operating modern technology systems.

Through cloud platforms such as AWS, students and developers can access flexible computing, storage, and data processing resources, supporting learning, research, and practical project development.

Key benefits of cloud computing include scalability, cost optimization, resource management, and faster application deployment.

### 5.2. Artificial Intelligence

Artificial intelligence is an important area of technology with applications in data analysis, software development, workflow automation, and intelligent systems.

Learning about AI helps students broaden their technological perspectives, improve problem-solving skills, and prepare for changes in the information technology industry.

### 5.3. Combining Cloud Computing and AI

Combining cloud computing with artificial intelligence enables the development of intelligent systems that process data, scale resources, and address practical needs.

**Illustrative workflow for building an AI application on the cloud:**

1. Cloud Infrastructure – Provision cloud resources.
2. Data Collection & Storage – Collect and store data.
3. Data Processing – Preprocess and standardize data.
4. AI & Machine Learning – Analyze data and develop AI models.
5. Application Deployment – Deploy practical applications.
6. Monitoring & Optimization – Monitor and optimize the system.

This knowledge can provide a foundation for student research and projects combining AI, Machine Learning, and Cloud Computing.

### 5.4. Community-driven Learning

An important value of AWS Student Community Day Vietnam 2026 is the connection between AWS student communities.

AWS Student Builder Groups provide an environment where students can:

- Share technological knowledge and experience.
- Support one another in learning and research.
- Explore emerging technology trends.
- Develop professional and technical skills.
- Exchange ideas and collaborate on projects.
- Encourage a culture of learning within the community.

Participation in technology communities allows students to develop their own knowledge while learning from the experiences of others.

### 5.5. Career Development

Learning about Cloud and AI as a student helps establish a foundation for choosing an appropriate career direction.

Beyond technical knowledge, students should proactively develop independent learning, problem-solving, collaboration, and adaptability to technological change.

Participating in community activities is also a way to accumulate knowledge and experience for future studies and professional work.
