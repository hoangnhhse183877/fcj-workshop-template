## 4. Personal Reflection

### 4.1. Strengths

**Progressive mindset**

I am eager to learn and develop myself, and I am willing to accept feedback to improve my knowledge and professional skills. I recognize that continuous learning is an important factor in information technology.

**Professional conduct**

I am mindful of respecting other members of the team and maintaining a polite attitude appropriate to the workplace. I aim to develop professional conduct in my studies and work.

**Sense of responsibility**

I take responsibility for assigned tasks, strive to complete them to an appropriate standard, and continue to improve my work efficiency.

**Teamwork**

I recognize the importance of coordination, mutual support, and knowledge sharing among team members in improving overall work effectiveness.

**Professional knowledge and skills**

I had the opportunity to gain more knowledge of cloud computing, artificial intelligence, and technologies within the AWS ecosystem. However, I still need to continue researching and practicing to improve my ability to apply this knowledge in real-world situations.

### 4.2. Areas of weakness

**Ability to learn**

My ability to absorb and apply new knowledge remains limited. When dealing with complex technologies or technical concepts, I need to spend more time researching, practicing, and strengthening my foundational knowledge.

**Communication skills**

I need to improve my ability to present ideas, explain issues, and exchange information clearly and coherently in academic and professional environments.

**Problem-solving skills**

My ability to analyze causes, evaluate alternatives, and select appropriate solutions still requires further development. I need to build a more systematic and effective approach to problem-solving.

## 5. Needs Improvement

I need to develop clear study and work plans, prioritize tasks, adhere to schedules, and improve my ability to manage task progress.

### 5.2. Improving problem-solving skills

I need to practice analyzing problems, identifying their causes, exploring alternatives, and selecting appropriate solutions. I also need to gain more hands-on experience through practical exercises and projects.

### 5.3. Improving technical English

English is an important skill in information technology, particularly when studying technical documentation and using AWS services.

I need to improve my reading comprehension, expand my technical vocabulary, and practice communicating in English to better support my studies and professional work.

### 5.4. Increasing proactiveness and independent learning

I need to proactively explore new knowledge, study documentation, practice, and seek solutions when encountering difficulties, rather than relying too heavily on guidance from others.

I also need to develop consistent learning habits to adapt to rapid technological developments.

### 5.5. Strengthening AWS and Cloud Computing expertise

I need to continue studying AWS services, cloud computing principles, system architecture, security, and methods for deploying applications on cloud platforms.

Combining theoretical learning with hands-on practice will help me consolidate my knowledge, strengthen my technical skills, and improve my ability to apply them to practical projects.
